About Khanapara Teer

Khanapara Teer is an archery-based traditional game associated with the Khanapara area near Guwahati, Assam. Results are commonly referred to as First Round (FR) and Second Round (SR).

This is an independent information page. It is not an official Khanapara Teer authority or result counter.
